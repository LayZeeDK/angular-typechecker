## Open-item decision (item 1) -- policy resolved by research

Three parallel researchers investigated the Windows cross-drive path case:
- **Prior art:** emit-absolute-verbatim is the overwhelming norm -- TypeScript
  (`getRelativePathFromDirectory`), ESLint (JSON + SARIF), Biome, oxlint, Vitest/Jest,
  Roslyn all emit absolute; none redact per-result. Only CodeQL drops out-of-root.
- **SARIF spec/GitHub:** absolute is discouraged (uriBaseId is the sanctioned redaction),
  but that machinery is over-engineering for a rare case and ESLint-SARIF/Roslyn already
  emit absolute; GitHub converts-or-drops gracefully.
- **Reachability:** rare, Windows-only, and already a knowingly-accepted ASVS-L1 residual;
  blast radius safe (the human formatter does NOT share `relativizePath`).

**Decision (user-confirmed):** keep the absolute-path emission (matches the ecosystem +
our own compiler family), correct the over-broad comment, and pin with a test. No behavior
change. Full SARIF `uriBaseId` redaction remains deferred Phase-31+ territory.
