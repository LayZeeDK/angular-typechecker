Prove the Wave-1 rule metadata end-to-end over REAL cold-compiler fixtures -- all four
diagnostic families carry the correct tag, level, and help text when the actual Angular and
TypeScript compilers produce the diagnostics -- and then prove the SARIF-only boundary and the

external-template `.html` case that the coarse heuristic exists for. The additive audit is the
standing gate that lets this ship as a patch bump instead of triggering the v0.3.0 escape
hatch.

Output: family-tag assertions across four families in the SARIF integration spec, the two
regenerated integration snapshots, and `33-ADDITIVE-AUDIT.md` recording the git-diff verdict
versus the published `angular-typechecker@0.2.3` baseline.
