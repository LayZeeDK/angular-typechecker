      6. Return { rawDiagnostics: the union (leaf diags in walk order + any 90002), rootNamesCount (SUM -- Pitfall 5), skippedReferences }.

    Do NOT filter or dedupe inside the walk (Pitfalls 1 and 2 -- the single run-typecheck finalize does both once over the union). Follow blank-line-around-control-flow and braces-on-all-bodies style; ASCII-only.
