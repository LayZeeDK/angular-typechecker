Establish the D-02 single source of truth for the 18 extended-diagnostic members and the
DRIFT-01 completeness tripwire that keeps it locked to the real Angular enum.

compile assumption in the phase (A2 -- the deep-import specifier resolving the enum under
classic resolution).

type-level tripwire), plus the `tsconfig.drift.json` + `project.json` wiring so the existing
