a single source. `render-report.ts`'s `sarif` case (which currently throws `'Phase 31'`)
is replaced with `await import('./sarif-report')`.

report with humanized `ruleId`s, mapped `level`s, repo-relative URIs, 1-based regions, and
self-computed `partialFingerprints` -- while the verdict/exit code stay owned by the engine
(`evaluateResult`), never re-derived (D-07).
Output: `core/extended-catalog.ts` + its completeness spec, `core/sarif-report.ts` + the
SARIF-shape unit specs (VER-01 slice), the `node-sarif-builder` dependency, and the seam swap.

"from a realpath-normalized pathBase + path.relative". That predates Phase 30's shared
projection. Per D-13 + RESEARCH "State of the Art", the SARIF reporter MUST reuse
`record.file` from `toDiagnosticRecord` and NEVER call `path.relative`/`ngCodeOf` itself --
re-deriving would reintroduce exactly the JSON/SARIF drift D-13 exists to prevent.

`core/diagnostic-record.ts` (`toDiagnosticRecord`/`relativizePath`, D-13), and the
